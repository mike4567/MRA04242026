# COMING SOON!

**The NOAA Fisheries Marine Mammal Incident Reporting System.**

We are developing a new portal to make reporting strandings and injuries of sea lions, whales, seals, and other marine mammals faster and easier for the public and researchers. This critical tool will help us enhance response efforts and monitor marine wildlife health.

**For Immediate Marine Mammal Strandings or Emergencies, please contact the West Coast Regional Stranding Network at 1-866-767-6114.**
